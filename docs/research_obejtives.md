# Research Objectives

The objectives of this research are:

1. Design a real-time onboard decision-making framework capable of simulating
   multiple plausible future system evolutions under uncertainty.

2. Develop a risk-based evaluation metric that prioritizes safety and graceful
   degradation over nominal performance.

3. Demonstrate that counterfactual evaluation enables earlier and more reliable
   abort or recovery decisions compared to single-trajectory autonomy.

4. Validate that such reasoning can be executed on embedded hardware within
   real-time constraints.

Success is defined by measurable improvements in early detection of unsafe
conditions and reduction of late or catastrophic failures.
